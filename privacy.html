Privacy

Your lists stay on this device

Saved products, shopping baskets, searches and pending product sightings are stored in this browser. There is no account or cloud sync. Search and ranking run locally, without sending queries to an AI service.

Maps and links

Current location requires your browser permission. ZIP search is an alternative. OpenStreetMap supplies map tiles and receives ordinary requests including your IP address and the requested map area. Retailer and directions links open third-party services with their own policies.

Hosting and offline storage

GitHub Pages hosts this app and receives normal web requests. A service worker caches app files for offline use. This version includes no advertising or behavioral analytics.

Clear your data

Your browser's site-data controls remove local lists and cached files. Other apps on the same origin may also be affected. Removing a home-screen icon does not necessarily clear browser data.
