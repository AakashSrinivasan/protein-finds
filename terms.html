Terms of use

Protein Finds helps compare vegetarian grocery products and plan a shopping list. It does not place orders or process payments.

Check the package and retailer

Nutrition and ingredient references may be old. Product appearance, store proximity and saved sightings do not establish current inventory. Prices remain unknown unless a retailer observation is attached. Verify the exact package, allergens, dietary suitability and price before buying.

Demonstration mode

The optional demo mode contains sample records and prices for testing. They are not current shopping recommendations or live listings.

Your local lists

Lists are stored locally, not backed up to an account. Clearing browser storage may remove them. This app is not individualized medical or nutrition advice.
